Our Mission

WHY WE
BUILT THIS

"Not everyone can afford organized sport. Everyone deserves to play."

We coached youth soccer and saw families paying $400+ every summer just for their kids to step on the field. Thirteen-year-olds paying from their own savings. All while public parks sat empty nearby.

PUFA was built to change that. We offer a free organizing layer on top of public spaces — so anyone with a phone can find a game, create a league, or build a team in minutes.

No registration fees. No travel to distant facilities. No gatekeeping. Just athletes finding each other and playing the sports they love — ranked, recognized, and respected.

The Ranking System

YOUR SPORTING
REPUTATION —
EARNED

THE ADAPTED
ELO RATING

The AER is PUFA's proprietary skill system — built on the same mathematics that power chess world rankings, adapted for team sports with variable margins and honesty verification.

Every player starts at 1,000 AER points. Beat stronger opponents, earn more. Your honesty rating multiplies your gains — rewarding fair play as much as skill. Beginners get a boost to help them climb faster.
